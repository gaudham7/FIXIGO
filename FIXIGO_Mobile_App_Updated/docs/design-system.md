# FIXIGO Teal Ivory Design System

## Design intent
A modern local-services platform: warm, dependable, approachable and operationally clear. Avoid cyberpunk/neon styling and avoid monochrome minimalism. Use teal as the recognizable brand colour, ivory to soften the canvas, white for content surfaces, mint for supportive accents, gold for selective highlights, and semantic red/green for alert/success states.

## Shared principles
- Colour carries meaning; do not colour every component equally.
- Keep primary actions teal with high-contrast white labels.
- Use deep teal for high-emphasis hero and navigation areas.
- Use gold sparingly for price highlights and attention.
- Use red only for destructive/error/urgent states.
- Use green for verified/completed/success states.
- Cards have 16–20px radii, subtle borders and restrained shadows.
- Keep text dark and legible on ivory/white surfaces.

## Portal distinctions
- Customer: discovery and request-first; categories, request composer, bookings and support.
- Provider: opportunity and earnings-first; availability, jobs, work progress and professional identity.
- Admin: desktop operations workspace; dense tables, queues, verification, oversight and analytics.

## Interaction status
The present revision includes local UI interactions for portal navigation, customer demo request submission, provider availability, provider job acceptance and admin table filtering/search. These are not substitutes for authentication, server-side authorization or persisted business workflows.
